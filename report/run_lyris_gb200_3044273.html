Executive summary

On 300 held-out validation questions the fine-tuned model answered 96.7% correctly versus 51.0% for the untuned base model. Cosmos3-Nano-qwen3vl was fine-tuned with LoRA (rank 16, targets q_proj, k_proj, v_proj, o_proj) for 5 epochs, 625 optimizer steps, on 4 GPUs. Training loss fell from 0.290 at the first step to a steady 0.0328 over the final tenth of training (-89%). Validation loss improved every epoch, ending at 0.0353 (best 0.0353); the best checkpoint is epoch_5. The last epoch changed validation loss by under 5%, so the run has essentially converged; additional epochs would bring little. The job completed in 24 minutes of wall time.
